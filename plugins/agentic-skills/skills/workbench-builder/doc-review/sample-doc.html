How the loop works

Your annotation is stored against the exact characters you highlighted. The agent reads it from the terminal with scripts/review.py list, edits the real source document, then resolves the note — and this page updates live, with the card turning green and the agent's reply attached.

  • A comment is a question or a note on the selected span.
  • A redline carries a proposed replacement for the span.
  • Entities like — and & and → anchor correctly — the highlight lands on exactly what you selected.
